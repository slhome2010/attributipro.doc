# Units of Measurement

This tool allows you to attach units of measurement directly to the names of **Attributes**, **Values**, or **Duty Templates**.

The display style for units of measurement is selected from a dropdown list.

## Example for Values and Duty Templates

| ![Tools Unit](/img/tutorial/tools_unit_after_value.png) |
|:--:|
| *Display after attaching units* |

Units of measurement are attached to each **Value**, even if they are separated by a delimiter.

Note that the **Value** `1500` remains unchanged.

:::info  
Units of measurement must be stored in the metadata; otherwise, there will be nothing to attach.  
:::

## Example for Attributes

You can avoid *overloading* the view with information by attaching units of measurement only to the attribute.

| ![Tools Unit](/img/tutorial/tools_unit_after_attribute.png) |
|:--:|
| *Display in Attribute name* |

:::info  
Avoid using this tool if you intend to enable visibility of units of measurement only on the frontend.  
:::
